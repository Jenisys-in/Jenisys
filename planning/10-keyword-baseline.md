## What this changes

1. **Two service titles were retitled to match real demand:** the AI Automation page now leads with "AI Automation Agency for Small Business" (4,400 and 260 searches) and the Support page with "Website Maintenance Services and Support Plans" (1,600 searches, difficulty 5).
2. **Low difficulty across the board.** Almost every term scores under 32, so a focused site can rank without heavy link building.
3. **Cost and use-case pages are small but cheap to win.** Volumes are 10 to 140 a month, but the intent is high (`ai invoice processing` has a very high advertiser price), and difficulty is near zero. Build them after the main pages rank.
4. **Next measurement:** connect Search Console to the project once the sitemap reads correctly, then track the first five terms weekly.
